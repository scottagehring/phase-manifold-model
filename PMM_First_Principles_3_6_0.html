1. The PMM Event Architecture

PMM begins with one physical event: possibility becomes realized interaction, and completed interaction leaves a persistent record.

  • Before (B): Pre-geometric possibilities.
  • During (D): Active contact and generated geometry.
  • After (A): Completed persistent record.

One complete event is a Field Transition Unit:

Fₜᵤ = B ∘ D ∘ A

The physical correspondences remain B = Wave, D = Particle, and A = Cube. These are correspondences of the three phases, not replacements for their event meanings.

2. Minimum Enclosure and Exterior Grades

The c-order ladder is represented by the exterior grades of a three-channel minimum enclosure space:

c⁰ → c¹ → c² → c³
Λ⁰ → Λ¹ → Λ² → Λ³

Λ⁰ is seed order, Λ¹ is traversal, Λ² is relational depth, and Λ³ is completed enclosure. Because Λ⁴ vanishes in this representation, c³ is the terminal forward grade.

The compact FTU coordinate representation is:

Fₜᵤᶜ = (B,D,A) = ((c⁰)ₘₚ,(c¹,c²)꜀,(c³)ₖᵦ)

3. Closure Coordinate and Grade Weights

A normalized coordinate q ∈ [0,1] tracks the realized branch from entry to completed enclosure. Within the three-channel product-state ansatz, exterior-grade projection gives the cubic grade coefficients:

W₀ = (1−q)³
W₁ = 3q(1−q)²
W₂ = 3q²(1−q)
W₃ = q³
W₀ + W₁ + W₂ + W₃ = 1

At q = 1/2, the c¹ and c² weights balance. This marks the intrinsic handoff from B-facing outwardness to A-facing inwardness. At q = 1, W₃ = 1 and the branch reaches pure c³ completion.

4. Contact, Completion, and Record

The event sequence is:

B → @ → D → −@ → C₃ → A

@ denotes contact actualization. −@ denotes recession of active reciprocal contact as D approaches completion. C₁ organizes entry, C₂ the active path, and C₃ the candidate completion/persistence boundary.

At the first admissible c³ passage, C₃ writes the completed top-form record R_A into A. Accumulated completed records contribute to the persistent history field Φ:

first completed c³ passage → C₃ → R_A → Φ

The physical origin of the C₃ write boundary remains an open derivation target.

5. Action-Locked Angular Progress

For timelike D, the Action-Locked Geodesic Principle links progress along the shortest normalized enclosure path to accumulated D action angle:

|dθ| = dϑᴰ
dϑᴰ = dSᴰ/ℏ

For an uninterrupted forward passage from q = 0 to q = 1:

ϑ_cl,minᴰ = π
S_cl,minᴰ = πℏ

This is a shortest-path completion value. A branch that retraces accumulates additional action before its first completed passage.

6. Characteristic D Scale and Depth

The characteristic D length is:

L = ℏc/Eᴰ

Relational depth belongs to the c² grade. Its length readout is generated during D and vanishes at both entry and completed enclosure. Completed enclosure belongs to c³ and is not itself a residual spatial depth.

7. Single-Vantage Recoveries

The single-vantage sector develops primary FTU readouts and conditional recoveries of local D geometry, tetrads, connection and curvature, special relativity, the null sector, closure capacity, Shannon information, quantum Hilbert-space and Schrödinger structure, double-slit interference, and the Heisenberg relation. Candidate extensions cover reciprocal coupling, holonomy, and the proposed spin-half carrier bridge.

These results are downstream of the event core. Conditional recoveries retain their stated assumptions and do not redefine the native B → D → A architecture.

8. Multi-Vantage Gravity and History

The multi-vantage sector develops conditional Bell, horizon-entropy, FTU-Jacobson, weak-field GR, weak-equivalence, and Friedmann recoveries. It also defines the persistent history field Φ and explores candidate record-source bridges, a proposed horizon-scale a₀ᴾᴹᴹ = cH/(2π), and a conditional MOND/AQUAL phenomenology mapping.

PMM distinguishes the defined additive history measure Φ from any future covariant transport law for that field. A unique record stress-energy tensor requires a covariant action or equivalent constitutive completion.

10. Conclusion

PMM v3.6.0 places a compact mathematical core upstream of its recovery program. A physical event moves from possibility through contact-generated realization to completed record. Exterior grades encode the c-order ladder, q and W₀ through W₃ resolve the enclosure path, action locks timelike progress to a minimum uninterrupted completion action of πℏ, and C₃ marks the candidate passage from completed c³ enclosure to persistent A-side record.
