## 1. Purpose and the FTU Family

PMM proposes an underlying event architecture connecting quantum mechanics (QM), general relativity (GR), and information theory (IT). Its primary goal is that connecting architecture. Its secondary goal is to investigate force manipulation through depth; this is a research objective. Resolving known physics problems is a further possible outcome.

The FTU family gives different readouts of one complete event. Its members describe the event's grades, spatial content, time, mass, energy, action, history and downstream gravitational response. They are not separate events. The central ideas run through the family: **time is angular; space is emergent; depth is different; light is stillness; and Mₒₚ connects realized relation with motion.**

## 2. Fₜᵤ One Complete Event and Contact

Fₜᵤ = B ∘ D ∘ A

B → @ → D → −@ → A

B = Before = Potential → Quantum Mechanics → Wave → Uncertainty → Pre-spatial

D = During = Realization → General Relativity → Particle → Partial Certainty → Spatial xyz

A = After = Transcription → Information Theory → Cube → Full Certainty → Post-spatial

**@ = Wave Function Collapse / Contact.** It actualizes an admissible pair interaction and opens D. **−@ = Anti Contact** marks terminal D closure. C₁ governs entry, C₂ conditions realization, and C₃ governs what persists in A. The QM/GR/IT labels express PMM correspondences; the certainty progression is an event-state heuristic, not complete microscopic knowledge.

Each elementary pair interaction is one shared FTU. Its completion produces two separate endpoint Afters associated with the participant lineages, sharing one event identity and one event-level source energy.

## 4. Fₜᵤᶜ The Ladder of Light Creases and Stillness

**The Ladder of Light is PMM’s c-order map of one complete event:** c⁰ represents Before, c¹ and c² represent During, and c³ represents After. The constants mₚ, c, and k_B mark their phase creases.

c⁰ → c¹ → c² → c³

Fₜᵤᶜ = ((c⁰)ₘₚ,(c¹,c²)c,(c³)ₖᵦ)

mₚ anchors entry, c scales realization, and k_B converts the information readout into entropy units. These constants are phase creases in PMM; their accepted physical values are inputs. Both c¹ and c² are D grades, associated with two internal x-y vantages. The native event ladder ends at c³.

**Light as stillness:** c is D's invariant still reference. Null propagation has zero proper time without supplying a photon rest frame. This does not remove angular D progression. The scale/action-time identity L = cTᴰ_action applies on its identified branch; it does not assign every interaction duration or participant separation.

## 5. Fₜᵤˣʸ Emergent Space and Distinct Depth

Fₜᵤˣʸ = (ψ(x,y,z), xyz, xy)

| Before B | During D | After A |
|---|---|---|
| Potential configuration ψ(x,y,z) | Active realized xyz geometry | Retained completed xy readout |

B's coordinates describe potential content, not active B-space. Active depth ends at transcription; the retained A readout is information rather than continuing active spatial geometry. A's cube representation is not active three-dimensional A-space.

**Mₒₚ — Motion Operations Principle:** Space is realized relation, and motion is the evolution of that relation. B carries potential relation and motion; D realizes them; A retains the completed outcome. Its kinematic summary is:

Mₒₚ(δᵢⱼ) ≡ (δᵢⱼ, dδᵢⱼ/dt, d²δᵢⱼ/dt²)

Here δ is inter-participant separation and t comparison time. The tuple does not determine the dynamics. An FTU has no isolated physical realization; geometry is relational across events and lineages.

Internal depth zᴰ arises from the common scale of the two D vantages:

ρ₁ = √(x₁ᴰy₁ᴰ),  ρ₂ = √(x₂ᴰy₂ᴰ)

zᴰ = L = √(ρ₁ρ₂)

Depth is distinct from ordinary height and width and from δ. This geometry motivates the depth-and-force objective without establishing a force-control law.

## 6. Fₜᵤᵀ Time Is Angular

Fₜᵤᵀ = (Tᴮ,Tᴰ,Tᴬ)

Tᴮ = [ℏ/(mₚc²)]Δθᴮ

Tᴰ = d/c

Tᴬ = Δθᴬ/ωᴬ

The current candidate span assignments are Δθᴮ = arccos√Pₑ@ and Δθᴬ = −ln Pₑ@ for 0 < Pₑ@ ≤ 1. They refer to the same selected contact's original probability and are therefore coupled. The phase-action interpretation of these assignments remains candidate.

Time is angular in PMM, with D progression θᴰ running from entry to adopted closure π. Action supplies the common ruler. If Tᴰ = d/c is identified with Sᴰ/Eᴰ, the existing scale relation gives d = L conditionally. Proper time, action time and comparison time retain their separate definitions; the temporal metric is open.

## 7. Fₜᵤᴹ and Fₜᵤᴱ Mass and Energy

Fₜᵤᴹ = (mₚ, Eᴰ/c², ℏωᴬ/c²)

Fₜᵤᴱ = (mₚc², Eᴰ, ℏωᴬ)

The entries have mass and energy units respectively. The adopted D-to-A frequency transcription, together with E = ℏω, gives Eᴬ = Eᴰ within one event. Two endpoint records do not create two independent source-energy copies. Neither tuple alone determines the energy selected by an interaction.

## 8. Fₜᵤˢᵛ Action as the Common Ruler

Fₜᵤˢᵛ = (Sᴮ,Sᴰ,Sᴬ)

Sᴮ = ℏΔθᴮ,  Sᴰ = ℏθᴰ,  Sᴬ = ℏΔθᴬ

Each coordinate has units J·s. The vector lets PMM describe all three phases with one measure while retaining their different roles. During D:

dSᴰ = ℏdθᴰ,  θᴰ_cl = π,  Sᴰ_cl = πℏ

c¹ and c² carry two internal D vantages, not two independent D action contributions. The same coordinates support two distinct operations: the Ledger and the Cube.

Fₜᵤˡᵉᵈ = Sᴮ + Sᴰ + Sᴬ

## 10. Fₜᵤˢ The Candidate Action Cube

Fₜᵤˢ = Sᴮ × Sᴰ × Sᴬ

Fₜᵤˢ = ℏ³ΔθᴮθᴰΔθᴬ

The Cube is a candidate action-coordinate box product with units (J·s)³. It is distinct from additive action, the A record cube and ordinary spatial volume. Its three perspectives describe one event:

| Perspective | Candidate readout |
|---|---|
| Before | Prospective completed products for admissible contacts e, using Sᴰₑ,cl = πℏ |
| During | Selected-event growth with θᴰ, holding B and prospective A spans fixed |
| After | Completed reconstruction at θᴰ = π |

Prospective A spans are not records already transcribed in B or D. For fixed B/A spans:

dFₜᵤˢ/dθᴰ = ℏ³ΔθᴮΔθᴬ

At closure:

Q(P) = −arccos(√P) ln P

Fₜᵤˢ,cl = πℏ³Q(Pₑ@)

Q(P) is candidate information-weighted projective contact separation. Both factors depend on the same probability, so the completed product is not a new independent parameter. At P = 1 the Cube vanishes while a contact may complete and the Ledger equals πℏ. As P → 0⁺ the candidate product diverges; no divergent observable is asserted. The metric, invariant-volume interpretation and native measurable consequences remain open. Closure count is separate.

## 11. Fₜᵤᴴ and Fₜᵤᴴˣʸ History and Retained Spatial Content

Fₜᵤᴴ = (B,(B,D),(B,D,A))

This is the current Core's cumulative phase-history readout. It summarizes which phases have occurred; it is not by itself a complete memory encoding or a law describing every transfer of content.

Fₜᵤᴴˣʸ = (B,ψ(x,y,z)) → ((B,D),xyz) → ((B,D,A),xy)

The combined readout pairs that history with potential, active and retained spatial content. It explains why a completed record can refer to a past realization without keeping its active depth present.

Each participant lineage receives its own endpoint After for event α. The minimal numerical record is Rᴬ_α = {ωᴬ,a_cl,Iᴬ}, with Iᴬ = −ln Pₑ@. Its frequency and terminal planar shape support reconstruction of completed x-y scale and shape. They do not alone establish arbitrary wavefunction, phase or image-sequence reconstruction. Endpoint records accumulate in the respective histories Φ.

## 12. Fₜᵤᴳ Recursion and Downstream Gravity

The Angular Temporal Recursion Principle, introduced in 3.7.4, postulates:

Aα(c³) → Φ → Bβ(c⁰), with α causally prior to β

Completed history contributes to later possibilities. Coherent contributions can interfere when relative phase is preserved. The native history-to-B preparation map and phase-preservation mechanism remain open; video playback supplies a conditional record-driven example with present apparatus and energy.

The gravity readout belongs to the multi-FTU sector:

κ = (dθᴰ/dt)/(dθᴰ/dt)₀,  φ = −lnκ

Fₜᵤᴳ = ∇φ,  g = c²Fₜᵤᴳ

κ compares D angular progression rates using the same comparison time. φ is a dimensionless clock potential; its spatial gradient has units m⁻¹, and multiplication by c² gives acceleration. Accumulated records condition later realization through this relational clock-response architecture. Source extraction and interaction dynamics retain their stated open status.

## 13. Proposed Extensions and Reading Guide

Paired temporal/spatial Fₜᵤᵀ˒ˣʸ, spatial-entry Fₜᵤˢʳ, distance Fₜᵤᵈ, length Fₜᵤᴸ, information Fₜᵤᴵ and force Fₜᵤᶠ have been proposed in separate development requests. They are not adopted here as additional canonical family definitions. The 3.7.5 Core reserves the length symbol and explicitly adopts the Cube/Ledger notation distinction; other extensions require their own integration and precise definitions.

The family supplies a connected account of potential, realization and record. Recovery comparisons and prediction tests remain separate from definitions and adopted principles. No force-control capability or new empirical grade follows from organizing these readouts.
