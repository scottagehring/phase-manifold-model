## Release summary

Version 3.7.7 presents the Phase Manifold Model (PMM) through one physical event and the relationships among many events. A Field Transition Unit (FTU) passes through Before, During, and After: possibility becomes an interaction, and the completed interaction leaves a record.

The phase manifold is the connected geometry of this passage. Active three-dimensional space exists during During; Before carries possible spatial relationships, and After retains the completed geometry as information.

This release contains the technical manuscript in Markdown and PDF, a First Principles introduction, and a README. The README introduces the model, and the First Principles document explains its central equations.

## Scientific content

- Event definition and contact. One elementary pair interaction is one FTU involving both participants. Contact @ begins During, and anti-contact −@ ends it. Completion gives each participant its own endpoint record, with one event identity and one event-level source energy.
- Angular progression and phase quantities. The Ladder of Light assigns c⁰ to Before, c¹ and c² to the two internal During vantages, and c³ to After. The manuscript defines time, mass, energy, and action quantities across these phases. The additive Action Ledger and proposed multiplicative Action Cube have separate definitions and units.
- Depth, length, and distance. The two During vantages generate internal depth zᴰ. FTU Length is the traversal length in the time calculation; FTU Distance-Range describes separation between participant lineages. Their relationships carry the conditions stated in the manuscript.
- Time calculations. The time tuple has rank 3 in the nondegenerate interior when traversal length is an independent input. On the conditional length-equals-L branch, the active event also has rank 3; completed closure has rank at most 2. These results depend on the chosen inputs and event phase. 
- Relational geometry and history. The multi-FTU description includes pairwise contact, participant lineages, and accumulated history Φ. The Motion Operations Principle relates realized separation to motion. The Angular Temporal Recursion Principle connects completed records with later Before possibilities.
- Information capacity. The A-phase rail area and its horizon-normalized capacity are defined in §25.3. The horizon entropy normalization is imported; applying it to a general completed FTU is a stated correspondence.
- Clock response and gravity. The angular-rate ratio κ defines the clock potential φ = −lnκ, with acceleration g = c²∇φ. Clock Flux relations and the comparisons with Newtonian and Schwarzschild results carry explicit source, coupling, and geometric assumptions.
- Recovery and observational tests. The Recovery Summary identifies the status of comparisons with established physics, including the supplied electron-energy correspondence R15. The horizon acceleration-scale calculation gives a₀ᴾᴹᴹ(t) = cH(t)/(2π). The manuscript compares Hubble-horizon and constant-Λ branches, reports their normalization differences from the quoted observations, and labels its example uncertainty as illustrative.

## Formulation and inputs

The manuscript identifies definitions, adopted principles, conditional mathematical results, and proposed physical interpretations. Recovery calculations establish compatibility with an existing result under their stated assumptions. 

The Action Cube is a product of phase-action coordinates. PMM proposes a reciprocal null/light-channel interpretation for the two During vantages.

The calculations use stated physical inputs: a Before-preparation map, interaction-selection rules, source extraction from retained records, and the geometry of the participant network. The horizon calculation uses its horizon rate; the event-level calculation uses the rate assigned by P7.

The manuscript's Theory Status, Recovery Summary, and Predictions and Falsification Tests state the assumptions and equations for these calculations.
