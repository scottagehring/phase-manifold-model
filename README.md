# Phase Manifold Model

> **Time is angular. Space is emergent relational motion. Depth is different. c is stillness.**

The **Phase Manifold Model (PMM)** proposes an underlying event architecture connecting quantum mechanics (QM), general relativity (GR), and information theory (IT). It describes one physical event through Before (B), During (D), and After (A). One complete event is a **Field Transition Unit (FTU)**.

Version **3.7.5** restores the candidate **FTU Action Cube** under Fₜᵤˢ and names the separate additive accounting **FTU Action Ledger**, Fₜᵤˡᵉᵈ. Both act on the existing phase-action tuple and have distinct units. The Angular Temporal Recursion Principle introduced in 3.7.4 is retained.

## Purpose

PMM's primary goal is to provide the connecting event architecture. Its secondary goal is to investigate manipulation of force through depth. Solving known physics problems is a further possible outcome of these goals. Force control remains a research objective.

## Core formulation

```text
Before → During → After
Fₜᵤ = B ∘ D ∘ A
B → @ → D → −@ → A
c⁰ → c¹ → c² → c³
```

- **Before (B):** pre-spatial possibility; the QM and wave correspondence.
- **During (D):** active realization, relational motion, and spatial xyz geometry; the GR and particle correspondence.
- **After (A):** post-spatial transcription and persistent record; the IT and cube correspondence.

**@ = Wave Function Collapse / Contact.** Contact actualizes an admissible pair interaction and opens D. Anti-contact **−@** marks D completion, with C₃ governing record transcription. These are PMM's phase correspondences and architectural assignments.

One elementary pair interaction is one shared FTU. It produces **two separate endpoint Afters**, one for each participant lineage, sharing one event identity and one event-level source energy.

## Central principles

- **Emergent space and Mₒₚ:** the Motion Operations Principle treats space as realized relation and motion as its evolution. B carries potential relation and motion; D realizes them; A retains the completed outcome.
- **Depth is different:** two internal x-y D vantages generate relational depth zᴰ. Internal depth is distinct from inter-participant distance δ. Active depth ends at transcription; completed x-y scale and shape remain available as record.
- **Angular time and action:** D progresses through an angular action readout, dSᴰ = ℏdθᴰ, with adopted closure at θᴰ = π.
- **Phase creases:** mₚ anchors entry, c scales realization, and k_B converts the information readout into entropy units. The native event ladder ends at c³.
- **Light as stillness:** c is D's invariant still reference. Null propagation has zero proper time, without a photon rest frame; angular D progression remains distinct.
- **Persistent history and recursion:** completed endpoint records accumulate along their lineages and condition later possibilities. The across-event bridge is c³ → Φ → c⁰, connecting distinct FTUs.

## Single FTU and multi FTU

The **single-FTU vantage** describes contact, creases, c-order, action, internal D geometry, the space window, terminal closure, and transcription.

The **multi-FTU vantage** describes relational geometry, accumulated history Φ, normalized D-clock response κ, clock potential φ, and downstream gravity:

```text
κ = (dθᴰ/dt)/(dθᴰ/dt)₀
φ = −lnκ
Fₜᵤᴳ = ∇φ
g = c²∇φ
```

The native history-to-B generation law, coherent phase preservation, interaction dynamics, and active-source extraction remain research targets. Recovery distinguishes conditional correspondence from imported-physics compatibility; Predictions identifies prospective tests and rejected candidates. Detailed electromagnetic, information-reconstruction, and video studies are included in the Recovery file.
